# Session actions stay enabled during a logo write

## Summary

While the desktop app writes a startup logo, several buttons that start another session action still looked usable.
A user could add a meter, open a recording, record or export in the middle of a write.
Now every action that could reach a meter or switch the session waits for the write to end.
Reading the screen, the theme and About stay available.

## Requirement

### Steps to reproduce
1. In the desktop app, connect a KM003C and upload a startup logo.
2. While the progress says it is writing chunks or reading back, look at the top bar, the meter tiles and the view tabs.

### Expected
Nothing that starts another session action can be used until the write ends.

### Actual
Stage 4 device run, 2026-11-06: Add meter, Open recording, Record, Recorder options, Export and Export .db stayed enabled while the device selector, Disconnect and the rate picker were dimmed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-70X5YY.1: Regression: while a logo write runs, from its first chunk to the end of its read-back, Add meter, Open recording, Record, Pause and Resume, Recorder options, Export and Export .db are disabled in the top bar, in the overflow menu and on every meter tile, each with a tooltip saying a startup logo write is running.
- AC-70X5YY.2: The keyboard shortcuts for those actions do nothing during the write.
- AC-70X5YY.3: The PD analyzer, Trigger, Records and Firmware tabs are disabled during the write; the Meter and Startup logo tabs stay usable.
- AC-70X5YY.4: About with its switches, the theme control, the overflow menu itself and the chart's own view controls stay usable during the write; a recording that was running when the write started keeps running and can be stopped once the write ends.
- AC-70X5YY.5: Every action listed above returns to its normal state when the write ends, whether it was verified or failed, including while the view then waits for a menu save.

## Technical Design

### Root cause
The held flag from the logo write reached only the controls that send to the meter directly; recording, export, add-meter and open-recording read their own conditions.

### Fix
Fold the held flag into the record, export and add-meter conditions and into the open-recording action, the shortcut handlers and the tile controls, and add the Firmware tab to the tabs a write disables. Export reads only the app's history, yet it is disabled too: the write lasts under a minute and one rule is easier to trust than a list of exceptions.

## Testing

Vitest for the derived conditions and shortcut guards; Playwright on Chromium and WebKit, with the wide font, that holds a mock upload mid-write and checks each control and tab, then checks they come back.

## Notes

Found in the M7c stage 4 run. The saving phase stays unheld, as in the menu-save bug FR.
