# The device picker cuts its label short after the chevron change

## Summary

After the drop-downs got their own arrow (FR K1GZTR), the device picker came out narrower than its label needs.
The browser stopped adding room for its own arrow, so the picker lost that width.
The longest entry no longer fit at 1280 and 1600 px, in every browser engine and on every OS.
The picker now sizes itself to its longest entry plus room for the arrow.

## Requirement

### Steps to reproduce
1. Open the app (desktop or web) at 1280 × 800 or wider.
2. Pick "Demo device — noisy-negative" in the device picker.

### Expected
The picker is at least as wide as the label, its padding and the arrow (the layout spec's check), so the label shows whole with clear room before the arrow.

### Actual
The picker is about 9 px narrower than that check, in Chromium and WebKit, on macOS and on Windows CI (run 42522985713, `e2e/ui/layout.spec.ts:251`).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-350H0S.1: Regression: the layout spec's device picker test, red before the fix and green after it, passes unchanged. From 1280 px wide, every device picker entry fits: the picker is at least as wide as the entry's text plus its left and right padding plus 12 px, in Chromium and WebKit.
- AC-350H0S.2: The check in AC .1 also holds with a wider font than the system default.
- AC-350H0S.3: The chevron keeps its 10–12 px gap to the box's right edge (AC-K1GZTR.2), and the select-arrow spec passes unchanged.
- AC-350H0S.4: Every other drop-down is at least as wide as its longest option's text plus its padding and borders, so no option runs under the chevron.
- AC-350H0S.5: Where the top bar is short of room (900 px), the device picker still shrinks with an ellipsis and keeps the full label in its tooltip, and the bar keeps one row.

## Technical Design

### Root cause
With `appearance: none`, an auto-width select is exactly its longest option plus padding and border. The native arrow used to add width outside the padding. K1GZTR moved the chevron inside the right padding, so the picker lost that width and the layout check (text + padding + 12 px) fails by about 9 px.

### Fix
The select shares a one-cell grid with a hidden sizer that lists every entry's label, using the select's padding plus the chevron's 12 px. The `minmax(0, max-content)` column still lets the picker shrink under `fit-shrink`. The 7rem minimum and 22rem maximum stay. The shared select rule is unchanged.

## Testing

The layout spec's device picker test runs unchanged. A new spec in `e2e/ui` checks the wide-font case (AC .2) and that every visible select fits its longest option (AC .4). The select-arrow spec and the 900 px layout tests run unchanged (AC .3, .5).
