# Clear chart history clears what the chart shows

## Summary

Clear history emptied the stored session history but left the chart as it was.
Nothing on screen changed, so it looked broken.
After a confirmed clear the chart, the overview strip and an export now hold only newer data.
The meter keeps measuring and an open recording is not touched.
The two buttons get names and tooltips that say what each one erases.

## Requirement

Confirming "Clear chart history" erases the session history everywhere the app shows or exports it, while the meter keeps streaming; the stats and chart buttons say what they erase.

### Steps to reproduce
1. Connect the demo device or a KM003C and stream for a few seconds.
2. Click "Clear history…", then "Clear".
3. Look at the chart, then click "Whole session".

### Expected
The chart starts again from the moment of the clear; "Whole session" and the overview strip show only data recorded after it.

### Actual
Nothing visible changes: the live window and "Whole session" still start at 0 s and draw every sample from before the clear.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-5X154V.1: Regression: `e2e/ui/clear.spec.ts › after a clear the chart shows only data recorded after it` reproduces the reported failure; it fails before the fix and passes after it.
- AC-5X154V.2: After a confirmed clear, the live windows, "Whole session", the overview strip, the gap bands and the playhead show only data recorded after the clear, the time axis starts at the clear, and the meter keeps streaming.
- AC-5X154V.3: An export after a clear contains only the rows recorded after it.
- AC-5X154V.4: An open recording is unaffected: Clear stays available while recording, and the recording file keeps every row from before and after the clear.
- AC-5X154V.5: The stats button reads "Reset stats" with the tooltip "Zero the statistics (min, max, average, charge, energy). The chart history stays."; the history button reads "Clear chart history…" with the tooltip "Erase the chart history and what Export saves. The meter stays connected; recordings are not affected."; the confirm question reads "Clear the chart history? The meter stays connected and keeps measuring."

## Technical Design

### Root cause
The clear command empties only the backend history store (store.rs clear_history). The chart keeps its own copies: the live buffer, the session start, the whole-session frame, the gap list and the overview envelope. Nothing tells it about the clear.

The chart gains a clear entry point that empties those copies, drops any history answer still in flight and returns to the live window. The app calls it once the backend clear succeeds. The statistics stay as they are; Reset stats zeroes them. The mock backend already clears its history.

## Testing

Vitest: the stats controls call the chart clear only after a successful backend clear. Playwright: clear while streaming, then check the live window, "Whole session", the overview strip, the axis origin and the labels and tooltips; clear while recording, then stop and check the row count. Rust: an export after a clear holds only later rows, and a recording keeps all of them.

## Notes

Found in the hardware review of the reload build. The user also did not know what the button did, hence the new labels and tooltips. Clear is allowed while recording because the recording file never reads the history store.
