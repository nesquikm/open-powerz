# A failed WebUSB connect leaves the meter claimed

## Summary

When a connect over WebUSB failed, the page kept the meter open with its interface claimed.
A second try then shared the meter with a read left over from the first try.
That leftover read could swallow a reply.
After a failed sign-in or an unplug, the page now releases the interface and closes the device.
The next try starts clean.

## Requirement

### Steps to reproduce
1. Connect a KM003C over WebUSB and let the sign-in fail (web HIL W2, try 1).
2. Look at the device in Chrome: it is still opened with interface 0 claimed.

### Expected
After a failed connect the device is closed and its interface released.

### Actual
The device stays opened with interface 0 claimed (WEB.hil-20261108-1548, notes).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-G30K4R.1: When the driver's sign-in fails at any step (timeout, protocol error, REJECT), the driver closes the transport before it rejects, and the WebUSB transport releases its interface and closes the device.
- AC-G30K4R.2: The WebUSB transport's close releases the interface and closes the device even when the transport already ended on a disconnect or a failed transfer; a release or close that throws is ignored.
- AC-G30K4R.3: After close, a transfer still pending from the failed session can no longer deliver a reply to a later session on the same device.
- AC-G30K4R.4: Regression: a fake USBDevice whose meter never answers CONNECT ends with the interface released and the device closed after the connect rejects; a second connect on the same fake opens and claims it afresh. Both fail before the fix and pass after it.

## Technical Design

### Root cause
The driver's connect rethrew sign-in errors without closing the transport, and the transport's close returned early once the transport had ended, so neither path released the interface.

### Fix
Close the transport in the driver's connect error path; make the transport close release and close the device once, even after the transport ended; closing the device aborts the IN reads still waiting.

## Testing

Vitest over the fake USBDevice: claimed and opened state after a failed connect, a close after a disconnect, and a reconnect on the same fake.

## Notes

Pairs with the sign-in fix in the same milestone.

## Hardware evidence (web HIL rounds 2 to 4)

- [x] AC-G30K4R.1 and AC-G30K4R.2: round 2 (qa-runs/WEB.hil2-20261108-2223, W2) found the device closed and every interface unclaimed after about 30 failed connects.
- [x] AC-G30K4R.3: round 4 (2ad6cc0, qa-runs/WEB.hil4-20261109-0946, W2b) pulled the cable within about 0.5 s of Connect; the page said "The meter is disconnected.", the device was left closed with every interface unclaimed, and the next Connect after the replug streamed.
