# Info popovers for features and abbreviations

## Summary

New users meet many panels and terms like PPS, EPR or SOP' with no explanation in the app.
Small (i) buttons beside each section title open a short popup that says what the section does and what risk it carries.
Abbreviations get a dotted underline and open the same popup.
All texts live in one glossary, written in our own words and checked against our protocol notes.
A switch beside Hide serial numbers hides the (i) buttons.
Nothing here talks to the meter.

## Requirement

The app explains its sections and abbreviations in place, from one glossary, with keyboard-friendly popovers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-CW66T1.1: One glossary module holds every popover text, keyed by term, each with a short line and an optional longer line. Components pass only keys; no popover text is written inline in a component.
- AC-CW66T1.2: An (i) button sits beside the title of each of these sections: meter readout, stats, chart, ruler, segment marks, E.D., sub-chart, PD analyzer, records, trigger, cable, console, firmware, settings and recorder. The ruler, segment marks, E.D. and sub-chart buttons sit beside their own controls in the Chart options panel. Its popover says what the section does. The firmware, trigger, console and settings entries also state their risk: rewriting the meter's firmware, raising VBUS above 5 V, sending raw messages on the bus, and writing to the meter.
- AC-CW66T1.3: Each of these abbreviations that the UI shows is drawn as a dotted-underline term that opens the same popover: VBUS, IBUS, D+/D−, CC1/CC2, SPS, PD, PPS, AVS, EPR, SPR, SOP/SOP'/SOP'', VCONN, DRP, QC, UFCS, AFC/FCP/SCP, BC1.2, ΔT/ΔAh/ΔWh, E.D., Ah, Wh, PDO/RDO, VDM and e-marker. A listed term the UI never shows gets no glossary entry, and the FR Notes name it.
- AC-CW66T1.4: A popover opens on click, Enter or Space, and closes on Escape, an outside click or a second press of its trigger. Focus returns to the trigger on close. Escape that closes a popover closes nothing else.
- AC-CW66T1.5: Only one popover is open at a time; opening another closes the first.
- AC-CW66T1.6: Screen readers get it: the trigger carries aria-expanded and points at the popover, and the popover has a dialog role with an accessible name. An axe scan with a popover open finds no violation. Hover gives only a native title preview with the short line, and nothing that covers the chart.
- AC-CW66T1.7: The popover stays fully inside the window at 375 px and 1280 px wide, is readable in light and dark themes, and does not cover its own trigger when there is room beside it.
- AC-CW66T1.8: A "Show (i) icons" switch in the About panel that the top bar opens, beside "Hide serial numbers", is on by default and saved per app across restarts. Off hides every (i) button; the dotted-underline terms stay.
- AC-CW66T1.9: Every glossary entry records its source: a section of the in-repo protocol doc, the USB PD or charging spec it names, or "our definition". The E.D. text says it is this app's own measure. No text is copied from vendor material.
- AC-CW66T1.10: A test fails when the UI uses a glossary key that does not exist, or when a glossary entry is used nowhere in the UI.
- AC-CW66T1.11: A README line, the readme-features list and a how-to section describe the popovers and the switch.

## Technical Design

A pure glossary module maps keys to short, long and source fields. One InfoButton and one Term component render triggers; a small shared store holds the open key, so a second open closes the first. The popover is placed by a pure function from trigger and viewport rectangles, unit-tested at 375 and 1280 px. Its Escape handler stops propagation so open panels stay. The switch reuses the settings storage pattern of the hide-serial switch.

## Testing

Vitest for the glossary, placement and store, plus a scan of the components for used keys. Playwright on WebKit for keyboard, Escape, focus return and 375 px fit. A visual screenshot set goes to qa-runs/INFO-visual/.
