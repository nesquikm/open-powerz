# The built-in logo with lettering leaves an empty strip on the right

## Summary

One of the two built-in startup logos has the open-powerz lettering.
On the meter's screen its wave stops short of the right edge, so a thin empty strip shows there.
The other built-in logo fills the whole width.
Now the lettered logo fills the width too.

## Requirement

### Steps to reproduce
1. Preview or write the built-in logo with the wordmark.
2. Look at the right edge of the image.

### Expected
The wave reaches the right edge, as it does in the other built-in logo.

### Actual
The last seven columns on the right are empty.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-P747X7.1: Regression: both built-in logos have opaque pixels in their leftmost and rightmost columns.
- AC-P747X7.2: The lettered logo keeps its 200 by 200 size and its alpha of 0 or 255 only, and its look inside the old area is unchanged.
- AC-P747X7.3: The brand generator writes the fixed art, so a re-render keeps the fix.

## Technical Design

### Root cause
The lettered logo draws the wave scene shifted by 20 and scaled by 0.8, but the scene ends at 212.5. That lands at 190 on the image, about 193 with the outline. The size rule plays no part: 200 by 200 stays 200 by 200.

### Fix
Extend the scene's right end past the edge in the generator, so the wave bleeds off both sides, and re-render the SVG and PNG.

## Testing

A protocol test decodes both built-in logos and checks the edge columns, the size and the alpha values.

## Notes

Reported by the user. Not written to a meter here; a hardware tester checks it on the screen.
