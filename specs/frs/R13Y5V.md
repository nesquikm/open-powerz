# A replugged meter leaves a dead entry in the device menu

## Summary

After unplugging and replugging a meter in the browser edition, the device menu kept the old entry selected and added a second one.
Connecting to the old entry showed "Something went wrong inside the app."
The menu kept the unplugged meter on purpose, so it could follow the meter when it came back, but in the browser nothing followed it.
Now the menu moves the selection to the replugged meter, and connecting to a meter that is gone says it is disconnected.

## Requirement

### Steps to reproduce
1. Stream from a KM003C in the web edition.
2. Unplug it, then plug it back in.
3. Click Connect.

### Expected
The menu shows one KM003C, selected, and Connect reaches it.

### Actual
The menu shows "POWER-Z KM003C (1)" selected and "(2)"; Connect on (1) gives "Something went wrong inside the app." (web HIL run WEB.hil2-20261108-2223, W6).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-R13Y5V.1: When the picked meter leaves the device list after its session ended and a meter with the same serial number appears, the selection moves to the new entry and the old entry is dropped.
- AC-R13Y5V.2: A picked meter that leaves the list with no same-serial replacement still stays listed and picked while its session runs or has just ended with an error, as before.
- AC-R13Y5V.3: Opening a WebUSB or WebHID device that the browser reports as gone (a NotFoundError) fails as disconnected, with the "The meter is disconnected." message and its hint, never as an internal error.
- AC-R13Y5V.4: The desktop app's waiting-for-replug behaviour and its tests are unchanged.

## Technical Design

### Root cause
The session's device-list merge keeps a vanished picked meter after an error so a desktop wait can follow it by serial; the browser has no such wait, so the dead entry stayed picked. The WebUSB open passed the browser's NotFoundError through unmapped.

### Fix
In the list merge, follow a vanished picked meter to a new listing with the same non-empty serial. Map NotFoundError at open to the disconnected error.

## Testing

Vitest on the session's device-list merge and on the WebUSB and WebHID open with a fake device that rejects open with NotFoundError.
