# Three small web edition snags from the parity HIL

## Summary

Three small things in the web edition.
Over HID, Read on the Startup logo tab showed the HID sentence twice.
A session export after reconnects and logo writes had a few rows with an empty TEMP.
Open capture could stay dead when its file picker never reported back.
Now the sentence shows once, those rows carry the temperature, and a new click on Open capture always opens the picker again.

## Requirement

### Steps to reproduce
1. Over WebHID, open Startup logo and press Read.
2. Over WebUSB, stream, switch HID ↔ USB, run a logo write, then press header Export.
3. Trigger Open capture with a click the browser opens no picker for (no change, no cancel), then click it again.

### Expected
1. The HID sentence once.
2. TEMP on every row that arrived while the temperature was current, as in the record CSV.
3. The second click opens the picker.

### Actual
1. The sentence twice (status line and error line).
2. 23 of 12,623 rows had an empty TEMP.
3. Nothing happens until the page reloads.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-Z99166.1: Over HID the Startup logo tab's Read and Show stored logo are disabled with the HID note as their reason, and pressing Read sends nothing and adds no error line; the HID note shows once.
- AC-Z99166.2: Regression: a sample batch whose session times run more than the snapshot age past the temperature reading (a backlog after a pause, a re-based session clock) carries that reading on every row in Export CSV and Export .db, as the live batch and the record CSV judge it on arrival; a batch that arrives with no current reading keeps TEMP empty.
- AC-Z99166.3: Regression: a new Open capture or Import vendor log click while an earlier picker never answered opens the picker again; the earlier pick resolves as dismissed and changes nothing.

## Technical Design

### Root cause
1. `LogoState.read()` sent the read over HID; its refusal became `error` beside the HID note already shown as the reason.
2. The session's temperature log judged each sample by its own session time, which the session clock places at or after the wall clock: after a jump it re-bases to the later of the wall clock and the last sample, so a backlog read after a logo write or a reconnect ran past the reading's 2 s expiry. The live batch and the record CSV judge the reading once, on arrival, and had none empty.
3. `browserPickPdFile` waited for `change` or `cancel`; with neither, `App.svelte`'s `pdOpening` stayed set and every later click returned early.

### Fix
1. `LogoState.readDisabledReason` (the HID note over HID); the view disables Read and Show stored logo with it; `read()` returns early while it is set.
2. `WebSession.onBatch` records the reading only when it is fresh on arrival, current until the later of its expiry and the batch's last sample time.
3. Each `browserPickPdFile` picker resolves its pending pick as `null` when a new one starts; `App.svelte` lets a click through while an earlier pick is pending, and only the latest call clears `pdOpening`.

## Testing

Vitest: logo state over HID (read disabled, nothing sent, no error), session exports with a backlog batch past the expiry, the file picker superseding a pending pick.
