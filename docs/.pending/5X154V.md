## Reset the statistics or clear the chart history

Two buttons in the Stats panel erase different things:

- **Reset stats** zeroes the statistics (min, max, average, charge, energy). The chart history stays.
- **Clear chart history…** erases the chart history and what Export saves, after you confirm. The chart, "Whole session" and the overview strip then start again from the moment of the clear.

The meter stays connected and keeps measuring through either one, and a recording in progress keeps every row.
