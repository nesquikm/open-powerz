## Keyboard

Press ? for the list of shortcuts. Esc closes whatever is open: the menu, a dialog or the clear-history confirmation, one at a time.

In a narrow window, theme and About live behind the ⋯ button. Open it with Enter or Space, move with the arrow keys, Home and End, and pick with Enter or Space; Tab or a click elsewhere closes it. Closing About puts the keyboard focus back where you opened it.
