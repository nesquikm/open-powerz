## Power when paused, and the rate during a recording

Power is stored with the session history, so a paused, zoomed or whole-session view draws the Power trace and shows it in the hover readout, with the true highest and lowest power in each stretch.

While a recording runs, the sample rate is fixed: the rate picker is locked, and the number keys 1 to 4 show "Stop the recording to change the rate." instead of changing it.

The chart's status announces every view change for screen readers, for example "Paused: 1:20 to 1:30" or "Live: 1 min".
