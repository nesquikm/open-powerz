## Startup logo in the browser

The Startup logo view works in the browser over WebUSB (`WEB_FEATURES` switches the logo
upload on). Choose a picture (PNG, JPEG, BMP or a raw `.bin` logo, at most 16 MB) or a
built-in logo, check the preview, type the word to confirm and upload; Remove clears the
logo. As in the desktop app the write touches only the logo area, sets the startup duration
to 3 s (upload) or off (remove), and reads everything back; nothing is retried. Afterwards
the view says whether the meter saved its settings or needs a save in its menu. A HID
session cannot change the logo, and only a KM003C takes one.
