## Move around a long session

The chart works like a video editor's timeline.

- **Overview strip:** under the chart, it shows the whole session. Drag its window to move, drag an edge to change the range, or click elsewhere on the strip to jump there.
- **Mouse:** the wheel zooms around the pointer; Shift+wheel or a sideways trackpad scroll pans. Click to place the playhead and drag to scrub it; middle-drag pans, Alt/Option-drag zooms to a range, and a double-click shows the whole session.
- **Keys:** ← and → move the playhead (Shift: a tenth of the view). J, K and L play backwards, stop and play forwards, faster on each press. + and − zoom, Home jumps to the start, End returns to live, and Shift+Z shows the whole session.
- **Zoom slider and presets:** the slider sets the span from 10 ms to the whole session; 10 s, 1 min, 10 min and Whole set it too and keep a paused chart paused.

Away from the plot, the readout shows every series at the playhead. Gaps longer than a minute shrink to a narrow labelled break, so the data on both sides stays readable.
