### Timing budgets in tests

Timing tests keep a strict budget on a developer machine and widen it by a fixed factor on CI, where shared runners are slower. The 10-million-sample history test takes both of its limits from `historyPanBudget`: a pan step must finish within `HISTORY_PAN_BUDGET_MS` (100 ms), and the whole test within `HISTORY_PAN_TIMEOUT_MS` (5 s). With the `CI` environment variable set, both are three times larger. A failure message names the budget it used.
