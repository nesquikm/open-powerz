## Small things that stay put

The chart remembers the window you picked (10 s, 1 min, 10 min or Whole session) and restores it the next time the app loads. Notices about saved recordings and exports stack in the bottom-right corner, so the chart never moves when one appears. The hover readout shows a single value when the lowest and highest values in a stretch round to the same number.
