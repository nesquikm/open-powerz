## Pause the chart

While the chart follows the newest data, its Live button reads "Live" and is pressed.
Click it to pause the chart on what it shows: the stream and any recording keep running.
Zooming or panning the chart pauses it too.

While paused, the button reads "Paused · Go live". Click it, or press End, to follow the newest data again.
